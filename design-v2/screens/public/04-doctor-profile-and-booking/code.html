Professional Biography

Dr. Ahmed Fathy is an esteemed Consultant in Adult Cardiology and Interventional Therapeutics. With nearly two decades of clinical mastery in both the Egyptian National Heart Institute and leading private UK tertiary trusts, Dr. Fathy integrates precision diagnostics with empathetic lifestyle counseling. He oversees comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessments, resistant hypertension regimens, and complex ischemic heart workups.
